Before you drop a file

Questions people ask first.

Where does my file go?

Nowhere at first. Your browser reads the file and builds the coverage report on your screen. We see rows only when you ask for the coverage plan, and only to match them against the ledger.

How accurate are the revenue bands?

They are modeled estimates built from review velocity, price and category rank history. Treat them as a way to rank sellers, not as reported revenue.

What happens if an email bounces?

Send us the bounce report within 30 days of delivery and every hard bounced record is replaced with a fresh one that matches the same filters.

Where does the data come from?

Public storefronts, mandatory seller disclosures, company registries and business websites. No data is taken from behind a marketplace login.
