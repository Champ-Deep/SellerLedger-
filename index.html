How a record is built

From storefront to verified contact.

Every record goes through the same four steps, in this order, and is refreshed monthly.

Step 1 · Discover

Index the storefront

Seller ID, storefront URL, listings, ratings and category come from public marketplace pages and store platform data.

Step 2 · Resolve

Match to a company

The seller's disclosed legal name and address are matched to a registered business and its own website domain.

Step 3 · Contact

Find the decision maker

Founder, owner or ecommerce lead, with a work email checked at the mail server and a LinkedIn profile.

Step 4 · Refresh

Recheck every 30 days

Sellers with no new ratings or listings in 90 days are flagged inactive and dropped from default counts.

Compliance

Where the data comes from, and how you can use it.

Sources

Public storefronts, mandatory seller disclosures, company registries and business websites. No data is taken from behind a marketplace login.

US

Contacts are business contacts. Outreach must follow CAN-SPAM: honest subject lines, a physical address and a working unsubscribe.

EU and UK

Records for EU and UK sellers are supplied under legitimate interest with a documented assessment. Personal contact fields can be excluded at checkout.

Opt-out

Any seller can remove their record through a public form. Removals propagate to all customers at the next refresh.

Questions buyers ask
How accurate are the revenue bands?

They are modelled estimates built from review velocity, price and category rank history. Treat them as a way to rank sellers, not as reported revenue.

Can I get Amazon 1P vendors as well as 3P sellers?

Yes. Brands that sell to Amazon wholesale are tagged as 1P vendors and can be added as a filter on request.

What happens if an email bounces?

Send us the bounce report within 30 days of delivery and every hard-bounced record is replaced with a fresh one that matches the same filters.

Do you cover India and the Gulf?

Yes. Amazon.in, Flipkart, Amazon.ae and noon are covered, with company matching against Indian MCA and UAE trade licence records where available.

How fast is delivery?

Lists up to 50,000 records are delivered the same business day. Larger or custom-filtered lists take up to three days.
